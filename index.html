Understanding UAE End of Service Gratuity (ESG)

End of Service Gratuity (ESG) is a monetary payout provided to employees who complete their employment contracts in the United Arab Emirates. Under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021, gratuity calculation rules apply equally across private sector entities in all emirates.

How Gratuity is Calculated in the UAE

Gratuity is computed strictly using your Monthly Basic Salary. Allowances for housing, transport, utilities, or bonuses are excluded from this baseline.

  • Daily Basic Salary Formula: Monthly Basic Salary ÷ 30 days.
  • 1 to 5 Years of Service: Entitled to 21 days' basic salary for each completed year.
  • Above 5 Years of Service: Entitled to 30 days' basic salary for each additional year beyond 5 years.
  • Maximum Legal Limit: Total gratuity cannot exceed 2 years' (24 months') total basic salary.

Eligibility Criteria for Gratuity Pay

To qualify for End of Service Gratuity in the UAE private sector:

  • An employee must complete at least 1 full year (12 continuous months) of service.
  • Unpaid leave days are deducted when calculating total service length.
  • Under current regulations, there is no longer a distinction between limited and unlimited contracts, as all employees transition under standard fixed-term agreements.

📖 How UAE Gratuity is Calculated

Under UAE Labour Law (Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021), End of Service Gratuity is calculated based on your Basic Salary only (excluding allowances):

  • Daily Rate Formula: Monthly Basic Salary ÷ 30 Days.
  • 1 to 5 Years of Service: 21 days' basic salary for each completed year.
  • More than 5 Years of Service: 30 days' basic salary for each additional year beyond 5 years.
  • Maximum Limit: Total gratuity cannot exceed 2 years' (24 months') worth of basic salary.

Note: You must complete at least 1 full year of continuous service to be eligible for gratuity.
